Design Notes

Muted tones, no icons on actions, subtle dividers. The mic icon sits quietly in the top-right corner — tappable but not attention-grabbing. Action labels use the same typographic hierarchy as Weather (large primary text, small secondary). No background colors or badges on the action rows.
